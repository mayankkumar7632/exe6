About Our Department

Established in 1995, the Department of Computer Science has been at the forefront of technology education and research, producing industry leaders and innovators for over three decades.

Our Mission & Vision

Building the future through knowledge and innovation

🎯

Our Mission

To provide world-class education in computer science, foster innovative research, and produce graduates who are prepared to tackle real-world challenges in technology and engineering.

👁

Our Vision

To be a globally recognized department known for excellence in teaching, groundbreaking research, and meaningful contributions to the advancement of computer science and technology.

❤️

Core Values

Integrity, innovation, inclusivity, and impact. We believe in creating an environment where every student can thrive and reach their full potential in the world of technology.
